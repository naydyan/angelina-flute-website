About Angelina

A performer who loves to teach

“Good teaching gives students the tools to keep growing long after the lesson ends.”

Angelina Yuan-Fen Ho is a classical flutist and private flute teacher based in Redwood City. She holds a Master of Music in Flute Performance from the San Francisco Conservatory of Music, where she studied with Timothy Day, former Principal Flute of the San Francisco Symphony. She also completed graduate study at the University of Maryland and earned her Bachelor of Arts in Music at UC Berkeley.

Along the way she has learned from some of today's leading flutists, including Denis Bouriakov (Principal Flute, Los Angeles Philharmonic), Mark Sparks, Emily Skala, and Isabelle Chapuis Starr, and has played in masterclasses with artists such as Robert Langevin, Paula Robison, Jim Walker and Marina Piccinini.

Angelina performs regularly with Bay Area ensembles — including the Berkeley Symphony, Stockton Symphony and Merced Symphony — and has played in education concerts designed for young audiences. She has taught privately since 2021, coached young flutists as an intern with the San Francisco Symphony's Education Department, and served as a woodwind teaching artist with Music Mission San Francisco. An accomplished pianist as well, she brings a well-rounded musicianship to every lesson.

FAQ

Common questions

What age can my child start flute?

Many children are ready around ages 8–10, when their hands can comfortably reach the keys and they have enough breath support. If your child is younger and very eager, get in touch and we can talk about whether they're ready.

My child has never played an instrument. Is that OK?

Absolutely. No experience or music reading is needed — we'll start from the very beginning.

Do we need to buy a flute first?

Not necessarily. Many families start by renting. Feel free to ask before you rent or buy — Angelina is happy to help you find a good student instrument.

How much should my child practice?

For beginners, a short, focused practice most days of the week works far better than one long session. Angelina will suggest a realistic routine based on your child's age and goals.

Can lessons be taught in Mandarin?

Yes. Angelina is a native Mandarin speaker and teaches in either English or Mandarin — whichever is most comfortable for your child and your family.
